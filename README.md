# Privacy Policy for Word Search

**Last Updated:** August 18, 2026

Thank you for playing Word Search! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Local Game Data
Our game is designed to be played offline. All of your core game data—including your level progress, coin/star balance, achievements, and settings (such as sound preferences)—is saved **locally on your device**. We do not transmit, collect, or store this gameplay data on any external servers. If you uninstall the app or clear your app data, your local game progress will be permanently deleted.

### B. Automatically Collected Information (Third-Party Services)
While we do not collect personal information directly, our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services collect data such as your IP address, device identifiers (like Advertising IDs), and general location data to function properly.

## 2. Third-Party Services Used

### A. Advertising (Google AdMob)
We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements on the home screen and provide optional rewarded video ads (e.g., watching an ad to earn extra stars or hints). 
- AdMob uses device identifiers and cookies to serve personalized ads based on your interests.
- For more information on how Google uses data, please visit the [Google Privacy & Terms site](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### B. In-App Purchases
We offer in-app purchases (such as Coin Packs) through your device's respective app store (Google Play Store or Apple App Store). 
- All payment processing is securely handled by the app store.
- We do not have access to, nor do we collect or store, your credit card details or billing information. 

### C. In-App Reviews
We use native in-app review tools to occasionally ask for your feedback. These tools are provided directly by the OS (Android/iOS) and do not collect any personal data outside of your standard app store interactions.

## 3. How We Use Information
Any data collected by third-party services (like AdMob) is used strictly for:
- Serving advertisements and allowing you to earn in-game rewards.
- Analyzing app performance and ad engagement.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our application is designed for general audiences. However, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 5. Security
We value your trust in playing our game. Since your core gameplay data is stored locally on your device, we recommend using standard device security features (such as screen locks) to protect your local data.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
