# Privacy Policy for Word Search: Brain Puzzle Game

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

Thank you for playing Word Search: Brain Puzzle Game! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Local Game Data
Our game is designed with a privacy-first approach. All of your core gameplay data—including completed levels, puzzle board states, coin balances, stars earned, unlocked achievements, and personal preferences (such as sound effects and theme selections)—is stored **locally on your device** using an embedded local database (Isar). 
- We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name or email) to play the game.
- We do not store your local game progress on any external cloud servers. If you clear your device cache or uninstall the application, your local game progress will be permanently erased.

### B. Automatically Collected Data (Third-Party Services)
While we do not directly collect or store personal data, our application integrates trusted third-party SDKs that automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable technical information to provide advertising, maintain stability, analyze gameplay balance, and prevent fraud. This information may include:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID), internal app instance identifiers, and basic device hardware identifiers.
- **App Activity & Telemetry:** Screens viewed, game progression events (levels started and completed, time spent solving puzzles), in-game economy metrics (virtual coins earned or spent), and interaction with advertisements.
- **App Performance & Diagnostics:** Crash logs, stack traces, device operating system version, and technical diagnostic metrics.
- **Network Information:** Standard IP address transmission required for internet communications (such as querying dictionary definitions).

## 2. Third-Party Services Used

### A. Advertising (Google AdMob)
We use Google AdMob to deliver advertisements within the app to support ongoing development:
- **Banner Ads:** Persistent banner advertisements displayed on select screens (such as the Home, Level Select, Gameplay, and Settings screens).
- **Interstitial Ads:** Full-screen advertisements displayed at natural game transition points (such as between levels).
- **Rewarded Video Ads:** Optional, user-initiated video advertisements that players may choose to watch to earn in-game rewards (e.g., free hints, free coins, or score multipliers).
- **Ad Removal:** Players can choose to permanently disable all banner and interstitial advertisements via an optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads").
- **Data Usage:** Google AdMob uses your device's Advertising ID and device metrics for frequency capping, ad performance measurement, fraud prevention, and (where consented) personalized advertising. You can review how Google processes ad data by visiting the [Google Privacy & Terms site](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### B. Google Firebase Analytics
We utilize Google Firebase Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymous gameplay telemetry. This helps us understand player progression, balance puzzle difficulty, and optimize the user experience. The data collected includes:
- Puzzle start and completion events, along with the duration taken to solve puzzles.
- In-game economy analytics (virtual currency earned and spent).
- In-game achievement unlock milestones.
- Screen navigation and feature engagement.
*This data is purely statistical and is never linked to your real-world identity.*

### C. Google Firebase Crashlytics
We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application stability and debug technical issues. When an unexpected crash occurs, Crashlytics automatically collects technical diagnostics, including:
- Operating system version and device model.
- Time of the crash and the exact line of code that triggered the error.
- App state at the time of failure.
*Crash reports do not contain personal information and are strictly used to release bug fixes.*

### D. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store)
We offer optional digital in-game purchases (such as Coin Packs, Word Packs, and Ad Removal Passes) handled via the official Google Play Billing system.
- All financial transactions and payment processing are handled securely by Google Play.
- We do not process, collect, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or financial details.

### E. Free Dictionary API
To provide educational value, our app connects to the [Free Dictionary API](https://dictionaryapi.dev/) when you find words, displaying real-time word definitions.
- This feature sends a secure web request (containing only the queried word) to their servers.
- Like all web traffic, your IP address is visible to their servers during the request; however, no user identifiers or account data are attached.

### F. Native In-App Reviews
We utilize the official platform In-App Review API to occasionally invite players to rate the game on the Google Play Store. This process is operated entirely by the operating system and does not transmit personal data to us.

## 3. How We Use Information
The technical data and telemetry collected through our third-party services are used strictly for the following purposes:
- **App Functionality:** To deliver game features, record in-game achievements, maintain coin balances, and process purchases.
- **Analytics & Gameplay Improvement:** To assess puzzle difficulty, analyze time-to-solve rates, and refine level design.
- **Advertising & Marketing:** To serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads, and to measure ad performance.
- **Fraud Prevention, Security, and Compliance:** To prevent fraudulent ad interactions, protect against billing manipulation, and maintain app integrity.
- **Diagnostics & Bug Fixing:** To analyze crash dumps and maintain app reliability across all supported Android devices.

## 4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). In accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies, if we become aware that personal information has been inadvertently collected from a child, we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our partner platforms. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at the email below.

## 5. Security
We take the security of your data seriously. Because your primary game save data is stored locally within an internal database on your device, your gameplay progress is protected by your device’s built-in operating system security measures. We recommend keeping your device updated with the latest security patches.

## 6. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or California, you are entitled to certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- **Right to Access / Deletion:** You have the right to request access to or deletion of any technical data collected by our analytics or advertising partners.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where data processing relies on your consent (such as personalized advertising), you can withdraw your consent at any time.
- **Consent Management (UMP):** For users in the EEA and UK, our app integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to allow you to customize your ad privacy preferences upon opening the app.
- **Device-Level Controls:** You can also reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking directly via your device settings (e.g., `Settings` ➔ `Google` ➔ `Ads` ➔ `Delete advertising ID` or `Opt out of Ads Personalization`).
- **Local Data Reset:** You can permanently delete all local game data and statistics at any time by clearing the application storage via your device’s Application Settings or by uninstalling the app.
